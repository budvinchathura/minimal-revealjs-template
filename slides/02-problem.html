Problem

Problem statement

Context

  • Domain challenge with limited annotated data
  • Need for fast iteration in a small lab setting
  • Deployment constraints on memory and latency

Goal: a minimal pipeline that preserves accuracy and is easy to audit.

Key questions

  • Where does performance degrade?
  • What is the smallest viable feature set?
  • How do we keep experiments reproducible?

Related surveys highlight the reproducibility gap .

Constraints and signals

Data constraints

  • Small labeled set, high class imbalance
  • Annotations drift across collection rounds
  • Privacy limits sharing raw samples

Operational signals

  • Latency budget under 20 ms
  • Memory cap under 1 GB
  • Weekly refresh cadence

These constraints drive the design choices and evaluation metrics.

Research contributions

  1. Compact pipeline that preserves accuracy under strict budgets
  2. Transparent feature engineering for auditability
  3. Evaluation harness with reproducible baselines

Hypothesis

Lightweight models can match large baselines with targeted signals.

Impact

Lowered cost and faster iteration cycles for small teams .
