Model Formulation

Model formulation

Objective

We optimize a compact model with a regularized loss:

$$\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \ell(f_\theta(x_i), y_i) + \lambda \lVert \theta \rVert_1$$

Hyperparameters tuned on a small validation split .

Assumptions

  • Data drift is bounded in time
  • Features are normalized per batch
  • Loss is smooth around the optimum

Swap the loss or add constraints here.

Training

Optimization setup

  • Optimizer: AdamW with cosine schedule
  • Batch size: 128 (gradient accumulation x2)
  • Early stopping with patience 5

Training runs complete in under 45 minutes on a single GPU.

Regularization and stability

Regularization

  • $L_1$ sparsity for interpretability
  • Dropout at 0.2 for robustness
  • Weight decay at 1e-4

Stability checks

  • Seed sweep across 5 runs
  • Variance tracked per metric
  • Confidence intervals reported
